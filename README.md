Hello there, my name is McHorse!

## What did I do?

I have created a suite of mods that let people create cinematic masterpieces directly in Minecraft. [Blockbuster mod](https://github.com/mchorse/blockbuster/) (and its complementary mods) for Forge `1.12.2`, and [BBS mod](https://github.com/mchorse/bbs-mod/) for Fabric `1.20.1` and `1.20.4`! New version of BBS mod developed with AI, BBS 2, is available on Forge `1.12.2`, Fabric `1.20.1`, `1.20.4`, `1.21.1` and `26.3`. The videos made with my mods, on YouTube, let alone, must've made over 50B views. I've also made some [REPO mods](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/McHorse/) with AI.
